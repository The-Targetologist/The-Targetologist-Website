# Reference Site Audit — lab41.co

## Purpose of this document
This document exists to draw an explicit line between what we can learn from Lab41 (structure, tone, quality bar) and what we must never copy (their specific facts, numbers, claims, images, or exact copy). This is the same boundary principle used on the Mian Tayyab Steel project regarding oht.com.pk.

## Lab41's structure (for structural inspiration)
- Hero: headline + subhead + primary CTA ("Book a Free Audit") + secondary CTA ("See How It Works")
- Trust bar: client initials/avatars + "15+ clients served" + live-looking performance stats
- Logos: integrated platforms (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, HubSpot, Salesforce, Marketo, GA, Search Console)
- "What We Do": 6-service grid (SEO, Paid Media, Content Marketing, Email & Automation, Lead Gen Systems, Analytics & Attribution)
- "Our Process": 4 numbered steps (Audit & Strategy → Build & Launch → Generate & Nurture → Optimise & Scale), each with sub-tags
- "Why Lab41": 4 differentiator cards + a repeated stats block
- "About": short founder-led narrative + founder photo
- Bottom-of-page conversion form with qualifying questions (budget range, biggest challenge) before the ask
- Footer: services list, company list, legal links

## Facts that are Lab41's, not ours — DO NOT COPY
- "15+ clients served," "100K+ leads generated," "£1M+ revenue attributed," "95% retention" — these are Lab41's claimed numbers. Do not reuse these numbers, rounded or otherwise, for The Targetologist.
- Named founder (Alexander Read) and founder photo — do not reuse or imply an equivalent unless it's The Targetologist's actual founder with actual photo/consent.
- Named platform integration logos (Salesforce, Marketo, etc.) — only list platforms The Targetologist actually integrates with in real client work (per existing GHL/Zapier work: GoHighLevel, Zapier, Google/Meta/LinkedIn ad platforms — confirm exact list before publishing any logo).
- Their 6-service breakdown — The Targetologist has 2 services (Automation, Advertisement). Do not stretch our 2 services into 6 to visually match.
- Their exact process step names/copy — we can use a similar *shape* (a numbered process), but write our own step names and descriptions specific to how The Targetologist actually works.
- Currency (£) — Lab41 appears UK/enterprise-facing; The Targetologist serves US-based businesses per the current site footer ("Serving US-based Businesses"). Use USD framing throughout, not GBP.

## What's fair to take structurally
- The 4-step numbered process pattern (audit-shaped, build-shaped, run-shaped, optimize-shaped) — reworded for our own 2 services
- A trust/stats section shape — populate only with real, confirmed numbers when available; otherwise omit the section or use qualitative language ("ongoing client work across healthcare, recruitment, and retail") instead of fabricated stats
- A qualifying-question booking form pattern (helps pre-qualify leads before a call) — reasonable to adapt for our own Calendly-based booking flow
- Clean, confident, jargon-light copywriting tone
