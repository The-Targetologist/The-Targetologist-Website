# Project Brief

## What this project is
Rebuild thetargetologist.com (currently WordPress/Elementor) as a custom Next.js + Supabase site. This is the agency's own site — the same agency that does the work described below.

## The business
The Targetologist is a B2B revenue-systems agency serving US-based businesses. It currently offers exactly **two services**:

1. **Automation** — CRM and pipeline automation, lead nurture sequences (email/SMS), workflow builds (GHL, Zapier), booking/follow-up systems. This is real, ongoing delivery work for actual clients.
2. **Advertisement** — paid ad campaign management (Google/Meta/LinkedIn-style), ad strategy and execution for client lead generation.

Do not present more than these two services as current offerings. The current WordPress site implies four services (Lead Generation Systems, CRM & Pipeline Automation, Client Acquisition Workflows, Revenue Operations Setup) — this appears to be earlier/aspirational positioning and should not be carried forward as-is. Automation and Advertisement cover this ground; don't reintroduce a four-service structure.

## Target audience
Operators and owners at small-to-midsize B2B service businesses (recruitment/staffing, consulting/marketing agencies, financial services, retail/vending, home services, healthcare/medical services) who have inconsistent lead flow, manual follow-up, or underused CRM systems.

## Primary conversion goal
Book a strategy call (Calendly-style booking flow). This is the single primary CTA — not a lead magnet, not a newsletter signup.

## Reference site
https://lab41.co/ — used for **structural and quality-level inspiration only**: their process framing (audit → build → launch → optimize), clean proof-of-work presentation, and confident copywriting tone. Lab41 is a larger, broader-scope agency (6 services, enterprise clients, named stats: "15+ clients," "100K+ leads," "£1M+ revenue attributed," a named founder). **None of these specific facts, numbers, or claims belong to The Targetologist** and must never be copied as our own. See `02-reference-site-audit.md` for the explicit boundary.

## What "better than the reference" means here
Lab41 is built for a 6-service enterprise agency. The Targetologist is a focused 2-service shop. The right move is not to copy Lab41's breadth — it's to build a **confident, focused site** that makes 2 services feel complete and deliberate, not thin. Borrow Lab41's quality signals (clear process, clean visual proof, confident tone) without borrowing its shape.

## Real client work (for social proof — see privacy note)
The agency has real, ongoing client work in: healthcare/medical services, recruitment/staffing, apparel/retail, home services, and B2B agencies — covering CRM automation (GHL/Zapier), lead nurture campaigns, and retargeting/re-engagement campaigns. This is genuine, current work.

**Privacy note:** Do not name specific client companies anywhere on the site without their explicit written permission. Reference real work **by industry/category** (e.g. "Healthcare & Medical Services," "Recruitment & Staffing") rather than by name or logo, unless/until specific client permission is obtained. This mirrors the caution already established on the Mian Tayyab Steel project regarding named third-party facts.
