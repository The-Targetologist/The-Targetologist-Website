# Sitemap & Page Goals

## Pages

### `/` — Home
Goal: establish credibility fast, communicate the "system, not tactics" positioning, and drive to Book a Strategy Call. Should summarize both services without going deep — deep detail lives on the service pages.

### `/services/automation` — Automation service page
Goal: explain CRM/pipeline automation and lead nurture work in enough depth for a qualified operator to self-identify ("yes, this is my problem"). Include process shape (audit → build → launch → optimize, reworded). Real platforms used: GoHighLevel, Zapier (confirm full list before publishing).

### `/services/advertisement` — Advertisement service page
Goal: explain paid ad campaign management (Google/Meta/LinkedIn). Same depth/structure pattern as Automation page for consistency.

### `/about` — About
Goal: founder/team credibility, why this agency exists, how it's different from a "tactics for hire" shop. Real photo(s) if available; otherwise honest placeholder, clearly flagged.

### `/work` (or `/who-we-work-with`) — Industries / proof of work
Goal: social proof by industry category (Healthcare & Medical Services, Recruitment & Staffing, Apparel & Retail, Home Services, B2B Agencies) — no client names/logos without permission (see `01-project-brief.md` privacy note).

### `/contact` — Contact
Goal: real contact info + booking flow. Keep the existing GHL-hosted form embed approach if that's still the operational system in use, or replace with a native form wired to the same backend — confirm which before building.

### `/privacy-policy` and `/terms-and-conditions`
Goal: carry forward real legal content from the current WordPress site (these are actual legal pages, not marketing placeholders — do not regenerate from scratch without the existing text as a base).

### `/blog` and `/blog/[slug]` (optional — confirm before building)
Goal: if the agency wants a blog for SEO, structure per `09-content-and-database-model.md`. Not present on the current WordPress site — confirm with the business owner whether this is in scope for this rebuild or a later phase.

## Explicitly not building
- No 6-service grid (only 2 real services)
- No fabricated stats section — trust section stays qualitative/industry-based until real numbers exist
- No named client logos without permission
